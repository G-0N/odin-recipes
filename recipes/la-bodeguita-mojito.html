La Bodeguita's Mojito

Here's an English translation of the famous mojito recipe from Cuban restaurant 'La Bodeguita del Medio'.

Ingredients

  • Havana Club 3 Year Old White Rum
  • Soda water
  • White sugar
  • Fresh mint
  • Lime juice
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. In a Collins glass, add 1/2tsp. of white sugar and 1/2oz (14.2ml) of lime juice.
  2. Add sprigs of mint and 3oz (85.2ml) of soda water.
  3. Gently mash.
  4. Add ice cubes and 1.5oz (42.6ml) of rum.
  5. Stir and enjoy!
