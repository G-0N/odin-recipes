Veggie Tuna Sandwich

This is a simple, nutritious, and quick meal I make often when it's summertime. Easy to make and hits all the spots if you are a tuna and egg fan.

Ingredients

  • Bread of choice
  • 1 can of tuna
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 1 tomato
  • Lettuce
  • Cocktail sauce
  • Sriracha

Steps

  1. Put eggs in boiling water for approximately 6 minutes.
  2. While the eggs are boiling, cut avocado and tomato in slices. Cut lettuce to desirable size.
  3. Cut bread in half if needed.
  4. Drain the tuna and put it in a bowl. Add boiled eggs and cocktail sauce (as much or as little as you want), and mix until achieving a paste-like texture.
  5. At the base of the about-to-be sandwich, put the lettuce, followed by the tuna and egg blend, then the avocado, and finally the tomato.
  6. Drizzle with a bit of sriracha and cover with the other slice of bread.
