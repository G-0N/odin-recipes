Swedish Pasta

This is a recipe someone taught me when I lived in Sweden. It's a pasta and mince dish with a homemade sauce consisting of cream (or soft cheese), ketchup, and soy sauce. It may seem like a weird mixture, but the end result is delicious.

Ingredients

  • Pasta of your choice
  • Beef mince (or your preferred mince)
  • Soft cheese or cream
  • Ketchup
  • Soy sauce
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Cummin
  • Saffron
  • Oregano

Additionaly, you can add these to finish it off:

  • Grated cheese
  • Chopped parsley

Steps

  1. Add water and a ton of salt to a deep pan, put it on the stove and set it to high heat to boil the water.
  2. Grap a different pan for the meat. Set the stove to medium heat, break the mince apart, and season it with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and cummin. Use a rather small amount of salt, as the soy sauce is already quite salty. Stir well.
  3. Add pasta to boiling water and check cooking time; we want al dente.
  4. Once the beef mince has browned, add 100g of soft cheese or 150ml of single/double cream (I used to make this sauce with cream, but eventually switched to Philadelphia Low Fat), ketchup, and soy sauce. I eyeball the last two as I know how I like it, so find a balance to suit your taste. Keep stirring.
  5. Add a few strands of saffron and a bunch of oregano. Keep stirring.
  6. Bring down the heat and leave the sauce simmering until the pasta is done.
  7. Once the pasta is al dente, the meal is done. Add some grated cheese and a bit of chopped parsley on top and you are good to go.
